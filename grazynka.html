To My Love ✨

Estera, my gorgeous lady
you are the adorable girl
who stumbled into my life
when I least expected it.

You came in smoothly,
like a cool breeze
in the middle of autumn,
carrying the refreshing scent of nature.

Everything about you draws me in: the beautiful curve of your smile, your captivating green eyes, your grace, and your perfect height.

When you are smiling with pure joy, that is what happiness truly is. I love all the memories we have made and all we have pushed through. ❤️

When I look at you, my heart beats a melody composed just for you. You have a unique, beautiful soul connected entirely to mine, leaving
me with pure love every moment i spend with you ✨
